VARIETY OF FOODS AVAILABLE

  • CHILLI CHICKEN

    Chicken pieces stir-fried with green chilies, garlic, and soy sauce, offering a spicy and savory taste.

  • SESAME CHICKEN

    Deep-fried chicken in a sweet honey and sesame glaze, creating a delicious combination of flavors.

  • FRIED RICE

    Fried rice with shrimp, vegetables, and soy sauce, offering a delicious and satisfying one-dish meal.

  • MAPO TOFU

    Ma Po tofu, or sautéed tofu in hot and spicy sauce, is one of the best Chinese vegetarian dishes and is popular throughout China. The main cooking methods are boiling and frying.

  • CARROT DUMPLINGS

    Not all dumplings are stuffed with meat! These carrot dumplings are elegant and filling, making for a great vegetarian Chinese recipe.

  • BUTTER CHICKEN

    Tender chicken pieces cooked in a creamy tomato-based gravy, enriched with butter and various spices.

  • ROGAN JOSH

    A flavorful and aromatic curry made with tender pieces of meat, usually lamb or goat, cooked in a rich blend of spices.

  • TANDOORI CHICKEN

    Marinated in a blend of yogurt and spices, chicken is roasted to perfection in a tandoor (clay oven), resulting in a smoky and charred exterior

  • BAINGAN BHARTA

    Baingan bharta is a vegetarian side dish that is very popular in India, Pakistan, and Bangladesh due to its rich aroma and flavor.

  • BIRIYANI

    "it’s biryani. The origin of this aromatic mixture of rice, spices and meat is usually credited to the Mughal kings who once ruled the subcontinent, but it’s now a popular dish all over the country."

  • BEEF DRY FRY

    Beef is synonymous with Kerala cuisine and Beef Dry Fry is a speciality. Seasoned with pepper, the beef is deep fried until it appears blackened. It is one of a kind!

  • MEEN KUZHUMBU

    A tangy and spicy fish curry with tamarind, tomatoes, and a unique blend of South Indian spices, creating a deliciously bold coastal dish.

  • HYDRABAD BIRIYANI

    Fragrant basmati rice layered with marinated meat, spices, and caramelized onions, slow-cooked to perfection, delivering a rich and aromatic culinary experience.

  • COCONUT RICE

    Fragrant basmati rice cooked with grated coconut, mustard seeds, and curry leaves, offering a subtly sweet and aromatic flavor.

  • RAVA UPMA

    A quick and savory dish made with semolina, tempered with mustard seeds, curry leaves, and vegetables, providing a hearty breakfast option.

  • VADA

    Deep-fried lentil doughnuts with a crispy exterior and a soft interior, enjoyed as a snack or part of a South Indian breakfast platter.

  • DOSA

    A thin, crispy pancake made from fermented rice and urad dal batter, often served with coconut chutney and tangy sambar, a South Indian breakfast staple.

  • CHAAT

    Chaat is a term signifying a huge variety of Indian street foods, snacks, or small meals which usually combine salty, spicy, sweet, and sour flavors.
